# Code of conduct

NotesCraft follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and take constructive feedback gracefully, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, report it privately to the maintainer through the [Security tab](https://github.com/Kronbii/notes-craft/security) ("Report a vulnerability" works for conduct reports too), or by contacting the maintainer on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may block people from the project temporarily or permanently.
