# Getting help

- **Something isn't working:** in the app, Settings › Help and feedback › **Report a problem**. It opens a bug report with your app version, iPadOS version and device filled in. Or use the [bug report form](https://github.com/Kronbii/notes-craft/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Questions, ideas, showing what you made:** [Discussions](https://github.com/Kronbii/notes-craft/discussions).
- **Feature requests:** the [feature request form](https://github.com/Kronbii/notes-craft/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).

## Before reporting

- Make sure you have the [latest release](https://github.com/Kronbii/notes-craft/releases/latest).
- Search [existing issues](https://github.com/Kronbii/notes-craft/issues?q=is%3Aissue) in case it's known.

## Common questions

**The app stopped opening after a week.** Apps installed with a free Apple ID are signed for 7 days. Reinstall the same IPA (or refresh it in SideStore/AltStore); your notes are kept.

**Will updating delete my notes?** No. Installing a newer IPA over the old one keeps everything. Deleting the app deletes its notes, so back up first (Settings › Backup).

**Where are my backups?** Files › On My iPad › NotesCraft › Backups. Copy them to iCloud Drive or a computer to keep them safe. Restore in Settings › Backup.

**The app crashed.** Please report it. If you can, attach the crash log: iPad Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Analytics Data, entries starting with `NotesCraft`.
